What this is not. It classifies how reactions arrived, not what anyone meant. A cascade is not proof of groupthink and a trickle is not proof of independent thought — they are timing descriptions that make a conversation worth having. Emoji meaning is room-specific and does not transfer; arrival behaviour does.
